Games

Anyone who knows me personally can tell you about how much I love video games. Single-player narratives are my favorite type of game, however my exposure to games is very wide.

This page is dedicated to every single-player game I've beaten, sorted alphabetically, excluding "the" and "a". DLCs are included as sub-lists.

Where appropriate, I link a Steam review1 that houses my opinions on the game. A much younger me wrote many of those reviews, and while I polished their grammar and structure, the earlier ones may blabber on.

Nonetheless, you're welcome to reach out to me privately about any game on this list if you'd like to hear more :)

1: Since December 2024, I always write a review after completing a game. Before then, I wrote a review if I felt like it. That's why many games lack reviews.
